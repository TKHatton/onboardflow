# Recording Cue Sheet

Keep this open on a second screen (or printed) while you record. Listen through
headphones, don't chase exact words, just get roughly close and move on.

---

**Clip 1: "Employee onboarding eats fifteen to twenty..."**
Screen: title_card.png or the blank browser, address bar visible, nothing clicked yet.

**Clip 2: "Watch what happens before a single tool runs..."**
Screen: you've just clicked Start Onboarding, the reasoning text is on screen, no
steps have run yet. Have the form already filled in and submitted before this clip
starts, don't fill it in during this clip.

**Clip 3: "Each of these is a live tool call..."**
Screen: steps are filling in one by one, ending on "Workflow complete!" Let this
clip's runtime be your guide for how long to let the steps play out.

**Clip 4: "Same system, same code, completely different plan..."**
Screen: second onboarding already submitted (different role), reasoning and steps
for that one visible.

**Clip 5: "Everything up to this point was the HR side..."**
Screen: click 👤 New Hire right as this clip starts. Let the checklist sit on
screen for the first half of the clip, then click "Ask Your Onboarding Assistant"
and click a suggested question chip partway through.

**Clip 6: "The web form isn't the only way in..."**
Screen: switch to the terminal, run the Pub/Sub command right as this clip starts.

**Clip 7: "And here's the part that ties it together..."**
Screen: switch back to the browser, HR view, click Onboarding Activity.

**Clip 8: "Under the hood, this is a FastAPI backend..." through "...try it yourself."**
Screen: whatever you want to end on, the completed workflow or url_card.png.

---

## If you only do one thing to make this easier

Don't try to match narration to footage you already recorded. Record fresh,
audio in your headphones as a pace-setter, video silent. That's the whole fix.
